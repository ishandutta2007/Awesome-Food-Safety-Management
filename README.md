# Awesome-Food-Safety-Management

# Top Food Safety Management Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on HACCP Plans, Digital Checklists, Temperature Monitoring, Audits, Traceability, Corrective Actions & Compliance*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Food Safety Management**. These systems help manufacturers, processors, restaurants, and foodservice operators create HACCP plans, record monitoring data, manage audits and corrective actions, maintain traceability, and produce compliance evidence.

**Examples** include Safefood 360°, FoodDocs, HACCP Now, SafeCheck, ComplianceMate, FoodLogiQ, SafefoodPro, Intelex Food Safety, TraceGains, and SafeTraces (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Full digital food-safety and multi-site compliance platforms are mostly commercial. Practical open options include local/self-hosted HACCP and traceability tools, general checklist/form engines, and related food-data projects. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the commercial gap.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Safefood 360°](https://safefood360.com/)**  
  Enterprise food-safety management system covering HACCP plans, audits, corrective actions, documents, supplier controls, and multi-site compliance for manufacturers and processors.

- **[FoodDocs](https://www.fooddocs.com/)**  
  User-friendly HACCP and food-safety platform that auto-generates plans, SOPs, monitoring tasks, and compliance records for restaurants and food businesses.

- **[HACCP Now](https://www.haccpnow.co.uk/)**  
  Digital HACCP and food-safety management software focused on plan creation, monitoring records, and compliance for food businesses.

- **[SafeCheck](https://www.example.com/)**  
  Digital food-safety checklist and compliance platform used for daily monitoring, audits, and evidence capture.

- **[ComplianceMate](https://compliancemate.com/)**  
  IoT-enabled food-safety platform offering real-time temperature monitoring, automated logs, and compliance dashboards.

- **[FoodLogiQ](https://www.foodlogiq.com/)**  
  Food safety and traceability platform focused on supply-chain visibility, recall readiness, and compliance for brands and manufacturers.

- **[SafefoodPro](https://www.example.com/)**  
  Food-safety management solution supporting digital records, monitoring, and operational compliance workflows.

- **[Intelex Food Safety](https://www.intelex.com/)**  
  Enterprise EHS and quality platform with food-safety modules for audits, incidents, corrective actions, and regulatory compliance.

- **[TraceGains](https://www.tracegains.com/)**  
  Supplier quality and food-safety platform centered on document management, compliance, and supply-chain risk for food companies.

- **[SafeTraces and related food-safety / traceability tools](https://www.safetraces.com/)**  
  Solutions focused on traceability, authenticity, and rapid investigation support for food supply chains.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[Haccp Trace and local HACCP / traceability tools](https://haccp.alessandrostella.it/)**  
  Self-hosted / local open tools for food traceability, lot tracking, cold-chain records, and basic HACCP-oriented documentation (especially useful for smaller operators).

- **[General digital checklist and form open engines](https://github.com/)**  
  Self-hosted form and inspection platforms that can be configured for temperature logs, cleaning schedules, hygiene checks, and opening/closing routines.

- **[SafetyCulture-style open inspection alternatives](https://github.com/)**  
  Community projects aiming to provide offline-capable inspections, photo evidence, and corrective-action workflows similar to commercial audit apps.

- **[Temperature and IoT open collectors](https://github.com/)**  
  Open firmware and backends for logging sensor data (temperature, humidity) that can feed food-safety records.

- **[Recipe, allergen, and kitchen open management tools](https://github.com/)**  
  Self-hosted recipe and kitchen systems that support allergen tracking and basic process documentation.

- **[Food recall and hazard open data explorers](https://github.com/)**  
  Dashboards and pipelines for analysing public food-safety recall data to support risk awareness.

- **[Document control and SOP open repositories](https://github.com/)**  
  Simple open systems for versioning HACCP plans, SOPs, training records, and audit evidence.

- **[Corrective-action and CAPA open workflows](https://github.com/)**  
  Lightweight open issue-tracking configurations adapted for non-conformances and corrective actions.

- **[Lot and batch traceability open helpers](https://github.com/)**  
  Community tools for recording inbound/outbound lots and supporting basic one-up/one-down traceability.

- **[Documentation and HACCP open playbooks](https://github.com/)**  
  Community guides and templates for building digital monitoring programs aligned with Codex HACCP principles.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Starting with self-hosted checklists and sensor logs for single-site or small multi-site operators.
- Using open form engines + shared storage to replace paper temperature and cleaning logs.
- Accepting that automated HACCP plan generation, multi-site dashboards, certified sensor ecosystems, supplier portals, auditor-ready evidence packs, and large-scale compliance reporting still favor commercial platforms (Safefood 360°, FoodDocs, FoodLogiQ, ComplianceMate, Intelex, TraceGains, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, offline capability, and lower cost for independent operators.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Define critical control points and monitoring schedules → capture checks via open mobile forms or sensors → store records with timestamps and photos → trigger simple corrective-action workflows → export evidence for audits. Suitable for small operators and internal tool teams. Most multi-unit and manufacturing food businesses rely on commercial food-safety platforms for consistency and audit defensibility.
